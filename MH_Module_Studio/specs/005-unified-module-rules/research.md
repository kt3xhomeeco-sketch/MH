# Decisions
- Bounding-box face identity survives affine panel edits and does not depend on ephemeral SketchUp face IDs.
- Positive tangential overlap prevents corner/edge adjacency being misidentified as assembly contact.
- ATT formulas remain authoritative; geometric contact at one state cannot prove an invariant ATT relationship.
- Sparse elimination supports face offsets and centre equations without adding a CAD kernel.
- Native source independence requires actual model checks, beyond portable fixtures.
